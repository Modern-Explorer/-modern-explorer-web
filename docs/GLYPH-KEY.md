### Stanza 2 — "Were they the sky-people? Were they the old ones from the east?"

Two coined compounds (documented below):

| # | Char | U+ | Sign | Reading | Role in text | Attested? |
|---|------|----|------|---------|--------------|-----------|
| 1 | 𒇽 | U+121FD | LU2 | lú | person | Base word for both compounds | Yes — ePSD |
| 2 | 𒀭 | U+1202D | AN | an | sky/heaven | *lú-an-na* = sky-person (**COINED**) | Signs attested; compound coined |
| 3 | 𒈾 | U+1223E | NA | na | genitive suffix | Genitive marker in *lú-an-na* | Yes — ePSD |
| 4 | 𒆳 | U+121B3 | KUR | kur | mountain / foreign land / east | *kur* = the mountains, the eastern lands | Yes — ePSD; ETCSL |
| 5 | 𒌓 | U+12313 | UD | utu | sun / Utu / sunrise / east | Utu = sun god; sunrise = east direction | Yes — ePSD; ETCSL |
| 6 | 𒂍 | U+1208D | E2 | è | to exit / to rise | *utu-è* = the sun rising = the east | Yes — ePSD |

**Coined compounds:**

- **lú-an-na** (𒇽𒀭𒈾) = "sky-person" — our term for alien/non-human sky visitors. Signs individually attested; compound coined for this cipher. *Analog: lú-an "sky person" appears in some mythological contexts.*

- **lú-kur-utu-è** (𒇽𒆳𒌓𒂍) = "people of the mountain where the sun rises" = east-people / Denisovans. Fully coined; documented here as private cipher vocabulary.

### Stanza 3 — "The secrets lie beneath."

Sumerian: *ad-ḫal ki-ta gál*

| # | Char | U+ | Sign | Reading | Role in text | Attested? |
|---|------|----|------|---------|--------------|-----------|
| 1 | 𒅗 | U+12157 | KA | ka | mouth / voice / word | *ad* (word/voice) — KA reads as "ad" in some contexts | Yes — ePSD; semi-coined compound |
| 2 | 𒄬 | U+1212C | HAL | ḫal | to conceal / to divide / hide | *ḫal* = "secret" (ePSD: HAL = to hide) | Yes — ePSD |
| 3 | 𒆠 | U+121A0 | KI | ki | earth / place / below | *ki* = below, beneath | Yes — ePSD; ETCSL |
| 4 | 𒋫 | U+122EB | TA | ta | from (ablative case) | *ki-ta* = "from below / beneath" | Yes — ePSD |
| 5 | 𒃲 | U+120F2 | GAL | gál | to exist / to be / to lie | *gál* = "it lies / it exists" | Yes — ePSD |

**Full sentence gloss:** ad-ḫal ki-ta gál — "The secrets lie beneath." (lit. "The concealed word exists from below.")

**Note on ad-ḫal:** *ad* (KA) + *ḫal* (HAL) as a compound for "secret" is our coined reading; individual signs are attested. ePSD lists HAL = "to conceal, to reveal (by ceasing to conceal)."
