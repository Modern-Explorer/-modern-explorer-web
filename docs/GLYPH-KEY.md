# GLYPH-KEY.md — Two-Script Margin Cipher

**Private decoder document. Not served publicly.**

The homepage margin columns present two ancient scripts as a slow burn-through cipher.
This document records every glyph in order with its Unicode codepoint, transliteration,
meaning, attestation status, sources, and the Morse mapping.

**Rule:** column and transliteration contain exactly the same signs, 1-for-1.  
**§** marks coined compounds (signs individually attested; combination coined for this cipher).

### Stanza 3 — NEW: "Are there ancient settlements beneath the great sand dunes?"

Transliteration: *ı͗n niwt iꜣwt ẖr ḏww wr*

**§ Coinage note:** "the great sand dunes" is rendered as *ḏww wr* (the great mountains) — the closest attested Egyptian expression. Sand dunes in the Great Sand Dunes context are geologically and visually mountains of sand; ḏw (mountain) + wr (great) is the chosen cipher equivalent. Marked § in GLYPH-KEY.

|---|------|----|----------|-----------|----------------|------|-----------|
| 1 | 𓇋 | U+131CB | M017 | Single reed | ı͗ | Interrogative *ı͗n* ("is there?") | Yes — TLA, Gardiner M17 |
| 2 | 𓈖 | U+13216 | N035 | Water ripple | n | Phonogram completing *ı͗n* | Yes — TLA, Gardiner N35 |
| 3 | 𓀗 | U+13017 | A019 | Old man leaning on staff | iꜣw | Determinative for *iꜣwt* (ancient things, elders) | Yes — Gardiner A19; TLA iꜣw |
| 4 | 𓇼 | U+13296 | O049 | Village with crossroads | niwt | *Niwt* = settlement / town | Yes — ubiquitous; Gardiner O49; TLA niwt |
| 5 | 𓂋 | U+1308B | D021 | Mouth | r | Phonogram in *ẖr* (beneath/under) | Yes — Gardiner D21 |
| 6 | 𓇊 | U+1320B | N026 | Single peaked hill | ḏw | *Ḏw* = mountain § (cipher: sand dune) | Yes — Gardiner N26; TLA ḏw |

**Full sentence gloss:** ı͗n niwt iꜣwt ẖr ḏww wr — "Are there ancient settlements beneath the great [sand] mountains?" → "beneath the great sand dunes?"

### Stanza 2 — NEW: "Did our ways come from the Denisovans, or from the Neanderthals?"

Two coined compounds (§):

| # | Char | U+ | Sign | Reading | Role | Attested? |
|---|------|----|------|---------|------|-----------|
| 1 | 𒇽 | U+121FD | LU2 | lú | Person / people | Yes — ePSD |
| 2 | 𒆳 | U+121B3 | KUR | kur | Mountain / foreign land / east | Yes — ePSD; ETCSL |
| 3 | 𒌓 | U+12313 | UD | utu | Sun / Utu / sunrise / east | Yes — ePSD; ETCSL |
| 4 | 𒂍 | U+1208D | E2 | è | To exit / to rise (east) | Yes — ePSD |
| 5 | 𒇽 | U+121FD | LU2 | lú | Person / people | Yes — ePSD |
| 6 | 𒆳 | U+121B3 | KUR | kur | Mountain | Yes — ePSD |
| 7 | 𒃲 | U+120F2 | GAL | GAL | Great / large | Yes — ePSD; ETCSL |

**Coined compounds (§):**

- **lú-kur-utu-è** (signs 1–4) = "people of the mountain where the sun rises" → **Denisovans** — fully coined cipher vocabulary; signs individually attested. The Denisovans' homeland is Central/Eastern Asia (the eastern mountains).

- **lú-kur-GAL** (signs 5–7) = "great mountain people" → **Neanderthals** — coined; signs individually attested. Neanderthals as the dominant large-brained hominids of the mountain regions of Eurasia. Best-verified alternative to lú-kur-libir (LIBIR is not in Unicode 15 Cuneiform block as a discrete sign).

**Full sentence gloss:** lú-kur-utu-è [tur?] lú-kur-GAL — "From the eastern mountain people (Denisovans), or from the great mountain people (Neanderthals)?"

### Stanza 3 — "The secrets lie beneath."

Sumerian: *ad-ḫal ki-ta gál*

| # | Char | U+ | Sign | Reading | Role | Attested? |
|---|------|----|------|---------|------|-----------|
| 1 | 𒀜 | U+1201C | AD | ad | *ad* = word / voice / utterance | Yes — ePSD; *ad-ḫal* semi-coined |
| 2 | 𒄬 | U+1212C | HAL | ḫal | To conceal / divide / hide | Yes — ePSD (HAL = to hide) |
| 3 | 𒆠 | U+121A0 | KI | ki | Earth / place / below | Yes — ePSD; ETCSL |
| 4 | 𒋫 | U+122EB | TA | ta | From (ablative case) | Yes — ePSD |
| 5 | 𒃲 | U+120F2 | GAL | gál | To exist / to lie | Yes — ePSD |

**Full sentence gloss:** ad-ḫal ki-ta gál — "The secrets lie beneath." (lit. "The concealed word exists from below.")

**Integrity fix:** AD sign (U+1201C, 𒀜) replaces the previous KA sign (U+12157). The compound is *ad* (word/voice) + *ḫal* (conceal), not *ka* + *ḫal*. The AD sign is the canonical opening of this compound in ePSD.
